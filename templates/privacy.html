Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Spendly is built to help you track your personal expenses with as little friction as possible. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have over your data.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the core expense tracking experience, Spendly stores the information you choose to enter, including:

  • Account details — your name, email address, and password.
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates, and optional notes.
  • Session data — login tokens used to keep you signed in.

We do not collect financial account numbers, bank credentials, or any information you have not explicitly entered into Spendly.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to operate the features you expect from an expense tracker:

  • Displaying your expense history and category breakdowns.
  • Calculating monthly summaries and date-range filters.
  • Authenticating your account and protecting it from unauthorized access.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use your expense records for advertising or for training any third-party models.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored in secure databases with access restricted to authorized systems only. Passwords are hashed using modern algorithms — we never store your password in plain text.

While we take reasonable precautions to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You are responsible for choosing a strong password and keeping your login credentials confidential.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — used to render the typography you see on every page.
  • Hosting infrastructure — used to run the application and serve it to your browser.

These services may receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type as part of delivering their functionality. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Your Choices

You can view, update, or delete your expense records at any time from within the app. You can also request a full export of your data or the deletion of your account by contacting us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website.
